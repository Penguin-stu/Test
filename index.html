Photosynthesis

Carbon enters the living world! Plants use sunlight to turn CO₂ into food (glucose).

Sunlight + CO₂ + Water → Glucose + Oxygen
Release

Respiration

Living things break down food for energy and breathe out CO₂ back into the air.

Glucose + Oxygen → Energy + CO₂ + Water
Extra Release

Combustion

Burning fossil fuels (coal, oil) or wood releases ancient stored carbon as CO₂.

Fuel + Oxygen + Heat → Energy + CO₂
The Recyclers

Decomposition

Bacteria and fungi break down dead matter, returning carbon to the soil and air.

Organic Matter → Nutrients + CO₂

The Big Picture

Think of it like a bank account. Nature maintains a delicate balance between removing CO₂ and putting it back. Human activities are making too many "deposits"!
