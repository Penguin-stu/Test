Chloroplasts & Chlorophyll

Chloroplasts are tiny "food factories" inside plant cells. They contain chlorophyll, a green pigment that traps light energy.

Stomata

Tiny pores on the leaf surface. Carbon Dioxide enters here, and Oxygen exits.

The Biological Conversion

Photosynthesis is an Endothermic reaction because it absorbs energy (Light) from the environment to create Chemical Energy (Glucose).
